# Knape Associates: Company Documentation

Knape Associates is a Houston-based specialty representative and distributor of
industrial air-movement equipment, founded in 1949. The company helps engineers,
contractors, facility operators and equipment manufacturers figure out, specify,
source and procure the right fans, blowers, dampers, louvers, heaters, make-up air
and dust-collection equipment for industrial and commercial projects. Its real value
is not the catalog but the application knowledge, the network of manufacturers it
represents, its sourcing flexibility, and the technical support that helps a customer
avoid an expensive wrong decision in demanding markets such as oil and gas, marine,
power, wastewater, healthcare and heavy manufacturing.

This folder holds the complete business documentation, built from Knape's public
material and kept in lockstep with the lead-generation rubric at
`apps/leadgen/outreach/jev_questions.py`.

## Contents

- [business-profile.md](./business-profile.md): the complete company and business
  profile. What they do, what type of company they are, what they sell, the
  applications they serve, the manufacturer network, how they make money, how they
  acquire customers, the brand story, how this maps to the lead-generation pipeline,
  and an honest list of what is not yet known.
- [buyer-personas.md](./buyer-personas.md): the people who buy from or specify
  equipment through Knape, each mapped to the exact keys in the lead-generation rubric.

## How to use this

- For lead generation, these documents define the ideal customer, the disqualifiers,
  and the buyer priorities that the Jev classifier uses.
- For outreach copy, the personas provide the message angle and the trigger for each
  kind of buyer.
- For any future brand or website work, the profile is the agreed definition of who
  Knape is and what makes them valuable.

## Sources and caveats

Everything here comes from Knape's public material (their website, product and
manufacturer pages, and LinkedIn), as researched for this project. Claims that are
interpretations rather than stated facts are marked as inferred in the documents.
Internal financials, margins, the customer list and channel performance are not known
from public sources and are not invented. One open item to confirm with the client is
whether the market is regional (Texas and the Gulf Coast) or nationwide, since the
public site is inconsistent on this point.
